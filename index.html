Project Vikas: a free mentoring circle for young women in tech.

The project is named after my father, Vikas, who turned 61 this year. He pushed me to become independent and supported me along the way. Project Vikas is my way of passing that on.

The project

One hour a week, October to December

Starting Saturday 10 October and running to the end of 2026, I'm hosting a free one-hour mentoring circle every Saturday evening, Sydney time, for young women in technology. Some are early in their careers and want to move faster. Some are between jobs and want a way back in. The aim is practical: help each woman take her next professional step with confidence.

How it works

The Circle

Women who are working and women who are between jobs meet on the same call every Saturday evening, Sydney time. Each week I teach one topic, then we work through real situations from the group. You'll hear what's happening inside companies right now, and you'll build a network of women going through the same things you are.

What we'll work on

Coming into your own power, professionally

  • DirectionWork out what you actually want from your career and build a plan for the next 12 months.
  • VisibilityMake your work seen: your CV, your LinkedIn and the people who should know your name.
  • NegotiationAsk for the offer, raise or promotion you've earned, and hold your ground when you get pushback.
  • Breaking into AIThe skills that matter now in GenAI and agentic AI, and how to build them from where you are.
  • VoiceSpeak with authority in meetings, at work and in public, without shrinking.
Your mentor

Tanya

I'm a Forward Deployed Engineer based in Sydney, working with digital-native companies to take generative AI from prototype to production. I sit with their engineering teams and build alongside them, mostly on agentic AI systems that have to work reliably in the real world.

Over the years I've worked across production machine learning, voice AI, fine-tuning large language models, knowledge graphs and evaluating how well AI agents actually perform. I care most about building AI that people can trust, with clear guardrails designed in from the start.
