For Papa, on turning 61.

You pushed me to stand on my own feet, and then you stood behind me every time I did. In good times and hard ones, you never once made me feel I was on my own.

I can't give that back to you, so I want to pass it on. Project Vikas is my birthday present to you: a month of my time spent helping young women in tech grow the way you helped me grow.

The project

Three hours a week, for young women finding their footing in tech

Through October 2026, I'm setting aside three hours every week to mentor young women in technology. Some are early in their careers and want to move faster. Some are between jobs and want a way back in. All of them deserve someone in their corner who believes they can grow into their own power at work.

How it works

One circle, plus time one-on-one

  • The Circle60 minutes, every week, free for everyone. Women who are working and women who are between jobs meet on one call. Each week I teach one topic, then we work through real situations from the group.
  • 1:1 time2 hours every weekend, paid, limited slots. Four 30-minute private sessions a week, on Saturdays and Sundays only. Use one to dig into your own career: your plan, your CV, a hard conversation coming up, the next step you're weighing.
Who it's for

Two ways in

Free

Between jobs

If you're a woman in tech who is currently unemployed, the weekly Circle is free for you, every week through October. Losing a job, or not landing the first one, shouldn't decide whether you get support.

Circle free · sessions paid

Working professionals

If you're working in tech and want to grow into a bigger role, lead with more confidence, or change direction, join the Circle for free and book whichever 1:1 sessions you need.
